Pick the right day to land on the Moon’s south pole.

There the Sun barely rises and Earth hides behind the mountains. Land on the wrong day and your lander runs out of power or loses contact with Earth. moonseal shows, for any spot, the good days to land, from NASA data. And once it lands, moonseal makes sure nobody can hijack it.
